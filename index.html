Quick grammar challenge

Batas materi: Was/were di sini menjelaskan keadaan, sifat, atau lokasi pada masa lalu. Untuk tindakan seperti “menaiki wahana”, gunakan kata kerja past simple: We rode the roller coaster, bukan We were ride the roller coaster.

Was or were? Start with the subject.

Tinjau pola dasar lalu gunakan dalam konteks kunjungan ke taman hiburan.

GRAMMAR IN CONTEXT
Tujuan belajar: menggunakan was/were sesuai subjek; membentuk kalimat positif, negatif, dan pertanyaan; memberi jawaban pendek; serta menceritakan keadaan di fun park pada masa lalu. Ditujukan untuk mahasiswa tingkat bahasa dasar–menengah (A2–B1).
01 / BE IN THE PAST

Am / is → was. Are → were.

SubjectPast formExample
IwasI was excited.
He / she / itwasIt was crowded.
A singular nounwasThe queue was long.
You / we / theywereWe were at the park.
A plural nounwereThe tickets were expensive.

You tetap memakai were, baik untuk satu orang maupun lebih dari satu orang. Bentuk was/were adalah bentuk lampau be, bukan bentuk lampau semua kata kerja.

02 / THREE FORMS

Move be, not the subject.

Affirmative: Subject + was/were + complement.
They were happy yesterday.Mereka senang kemarin.
Negative: Subject + was/were + not + complement.
They were not tired yesterday.Were not → weren't. Was not → wasn't.
Question: Was/Were + subject + complement?
Were they happy yesterday?Pindahkan was/were ke depan subjek. Jangan menambahkan did.

Salah: Did you were at the park?
Benar: Were you at the park?

03 / SHORT ANSWERS

Match the answer to the subject.

Were you at the park?
Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.Jika ditanya “you” sebagai satu orang, jawab tentang diri sendiri dengan “I was”. Jika mewakili kelompok: “Yes, we were.”
Was the roller coaster open?
Yes, it was. / No, it wasn't.
Were the tickets expensive?
Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.

Jawaban pendek positif memakai bentuk penuh: Yes, it was. Bentuk negatif dapat disingkat: No, it wasn't.

04 / TIME & LOCATION

Put the memory in context.

Yesterday: We were at the fun park yesterday.
Last weekend: The park was busy last weekend.
Two days ago: The café was open two days ago.
Where were you yesterday?
I was at the fun park.
How was the roller coaster?
It was exciting.

Where menanyakan lokasi, sedangkan how menanyakan keadaan atau kesan. “Yesterday” tidak memerlukan in/on/at: bukan in yesterday.
